We're writing to share some news about the future of the Bytecode Alliance and the WebAssembly Micro Runtime (WAMR).

Going forward, the WAMR project will continue its development outside of the Bytecode Alliance.

As both WAMR and the Bytecode Alliance have grown, it's become clear that each has its own distinct goals and direction. After working through this together, we've arrived at the shared understanding that the project will be best positioned to pursue its own priorities as an independent effort.

We want to thank everyone who has been part of WAMR during its time with the Alliance, and we wish the project and its community well as they move forward.

The Bytecode Alliance remains focused on our mission of building a secure, capable foundation for WebAssembly, and we're excited about the work ahead.

The Bytecode Alliance
